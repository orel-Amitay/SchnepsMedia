Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Transfer (“the App”, “we”, “us”), presented by Schneps Media, collects, uses, and shares information when you use https://schnepsmedia.vercel.app/.

1. Who we are

Transfer is a free daily NYC subway line-guessing game operated for Schneps Media. Contact for privacy questions: orelamitay8@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Google account information (optional). If you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google shares with us a unique user ID, display name, email address, and profile photo URL via Firebase Authentication. We request this only when you start Google sign-in.
  • Gameplay and leaderboard data. When signed in, we may store your display name, photo URL, daily puzzle date, win/loss result, guess count, and score in Google Cloud Firestore so a shared daily leaderboard can be shown to other players.
  • Local device data. The App stores game progress, settings (for example dark mode), guest display name, and statistics (played games, win streak) in your browser’s localStorage. This data stays on your device unless you clear site data.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) and Google Firebase may automatically process standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps needed to deliver and secure the service.

3. How we use information

  • To let you sign in and keep your session across visits
  • To show your name/photo on the daily leaderboard when you compete
  • To remember puzzle progress, settings, and streaks on your device
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal bases / consent

Google sign-in and leaderboard participation are optional. You can play as a guest without connecting a Google account. Guest scores remain on your device only.

5. Sharing of information

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication and optional cloud leaderboard storage.
  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery of the App.
  • Other players — if you join the shared leaderboard, your display name, photo, and score for that day may be visible in the App.

We do not share personal information with advertisers. Service providers process data only to provide their services under their own privacy terms.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The App primarily uses browser localStorage for gameplay state. Firebase Authentication may use cookies or similar browser storage to maintain your signed-in session. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

7. Data retention

  • LocalStorage data remains until you clear it in your browser or reset the board/settings.
  • Firebase Auth account records and Firestore leaderboard entries are retained while the App is operated, or until you request deletion.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Play as guest without Google sign-in
  • Sign out from Settings at any time
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove local progress/stats
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your cloud account/leaderboard data by emailing orelamitay8@gmail.com

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International processing

Data may be processed in the United States or other locations where Google Cloud and Vercel operate servers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
